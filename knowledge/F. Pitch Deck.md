## 📊 Pitch Deck Structure

### Standard VC Pitch Deck Format

```
1.  Cover Slide (Company Name & Tagline)
2.  Problem (The Problem to Solve)
3.  Solution (The Solution)
4.  Product Demo (Product Demonstration)
5.  Market Size (Market Size)
6.  Business Model (Business Model)
7.  Traction (Traction & Momentum)
8.  Competition (Competitive Comparison)
9.  Competitive Advantage (Competitive Advantages)
10. Go-to-Market Strategy (Market Entry Strategy)
11. Team (Team)
12. Financials (Financial Projections)
13. Unit Economics (Unit Economics)
14. Use of Funds (Use of Funds)
15. Vision (Long-term Vision)
16. Appendix (Supplementary Materials)

```

## 🎤 Pitch Deck Delivery Guide

### Presentation Tips

```yaml
Timing:
  - Total: 15-20 minutes presentation + 10-15 min Q&A
  - Spend most time on: Problem, Solution, Market, Team
  - Breeze through: Appendix (only if asked)

Energy:
  - Start strong (Problem slide should grab attention)
  - Show passion on Solution & Vision
  - Be matter-of-fact on Financials (confidence, not hype)

Body Language:
  - Stand, don't sit (if in-person)
  - Make eye contact with decision-maker
  - Use hand gestures (enthusiasm)
  - Smile when talking about vision

Handling Questions:
  - "Great question" (buy time to think)
  - If you don't know: "I'll get back to you on that"
  - Pivot objections: "That's why we're building X..."
  - Common Q: "What if [big tech] does this?"
    → Answer: "We welcome competition—validates market. Our moat is X."

Follow-up:
  - Send deck within 24 hours
  - Include: Executive summary (1-pager), Financial model, Data room link
  - Schedule next meeting before leaving current one

```
